# PECT-JEPA Research Invariants & Execution Protocols

## 1. Execution-First Research Protocol
- **No Speculative Proposals Without Code & Data**: Do not present theoretical proposals and leave execution to the user. When a new architectural or loss idea is considered, implement the code cleanly, run an isolated empirical test (minimum 3 epochs), extract downstream metrics, and present concrete empirical data.
- **Non-Breaking, Isolated Additions**: All new tokenizers, predictors, or loss formulations must be added as modular extensions (new factory options, new classes, or distinct configuration keys). Never overwrite, break, or remove previously working baseline implementations.
- **Transparent Data Reporting**: Every experiment report must include exact loss trajectories, intrinsic dimension (Two-NN), Anomaly Detection AUC/AP/CNR, and quantitative Depth Regression ($R^2$, MAE mm, defect-only $R^2$).
- **Continuous Git Synchronization & Checkpointing**: Whenever a new experiment or architectural addition is implemented, immediately stage, commit with a descriptive message referencing the experiment ID, and push code to the remote Git branch in tandem with updating `RESEARCH_REGISTRY.md` and experiment report artifacts. This ensures complete version control, atomic reproducibility, and frictionless rollbacks to previous codebases.

## 2. Research Tracking & Rejection Registry
- **Check Before Proposing**: Consult `RESEARCH_REGISTRY.md` before exploring or proposing any research direction. Never re-propose previously rejected directions unless addressing the specific root cause documented in the registry.
- **Empirical Justification for Rejection**: Any decision to reject a hypothesis or architecture must be substantiated with concrete experimental data recorded in the registry, not subjective speculation or superficial assumptions.

## 3. Physical & Data Invariants for PECT
- **Multi-Waveform Reality & Strict Prohibition of Temporal Slicing**: The dataset contains three distinct waveforms: Square, Gaussian, and Chirp across three sensors (Hall Air Core, Hall Pot Core, TMR), and real-world PECT inspection encounters arbitrary unknown excitation signals. Never assume single-pulse legacy PECT dynamics. NEVER hardcode chronological time-stage chunking or temporal slicing (e.g., $\tau_0..\tau_3$, slicing 128 samples into arbitrary discrete stages) under the assumption that early time is shallow and late time is deep. There is zero physical or empirical justification for such arbitrary intervals across diverse waveforms; in Chirp waveforms, time maps to excitation frequency ($f(t) = f_0 + \beta t$), completely inverting penetration depth order ($\delta \propto 1/\sqrt{f}$), while Gaussian pulses are centered wavepackets. Any temporal stage slicing is physically invalid, meaningless, and permanently banned. Tokenization must remain strictly waveform-agnostic: exactly one continuous token per spatial probe combining continuous 1D temporal projection and uncrushed Fourier harmonic decomposition.
- **Dual-Domain Invariant**: Both time-domain transient dynamics (peak arrival $t_p$, LOI point, energy integral) and spectral dispersion ($\Delta \theta(f) \propto d \sqrt{f}$) carry non-redundant physical information. Neither domain may be omitted.
- **Pure Self-Supervised Paradigm & Non-Contrastive Mandate**: Zero external metadata injection. The model must learn representations purely from the observed measurement $x \in \mathbb{R}^{5 \times 5 \times C}$ without supervisory flags (e.g., no waveform or sensor labels). Never inject ad-hoc synthetic perturbations (e.g., artificial lift-off attenuation) coupled with positive-pair contrastive or contractive distance penalties ($1 - \cos(z, z_{\text{pert}})$). Lift-off invariance must be achieved intrinsically via Dodd-Deeds Fourier Phase $\theta(f) = \arg(X(f))$, preserving 100% pure JEPA predictive learning.
- **No Minority Defect Oversampling**: Defect areas constitute only $\approx 1.2\%$ of spatial scans. Never force artificial 50/50 minority batch oversampling, which causes severe micro-noise memorization, distorts VICReg covariance geometry, and destroys out-of-distribution generalizability. Maintain natural, unbiased file-balanced sampling.
- **No Spatial Variance Heuristic Selection**: Local spatial variance cannot distinguish between mechanical probe lift-off/tilt and real defects. Never use spatial amplitude variance heuristics to guide sampling or define flaw presence.
- **Two-Stage Hurdle Protocol for Zero-Inflated NDT Evaluation**: In PECT C-scans, $\approx 90\%$ of evaluated pixels are sound metal ($y = 0.0\,\text{mm}$). Baseline probe lift-off fluctuations on sound metal must not be confused with flaw sizing failure. Benchmark evaluation must report detection (AUC, AP, CNR) and conditional defect sizing ($R^2$, MAE on $y > 0$) as complementary stages in a Hurdle evaluation.
- **Anti-Collapse Requirement**: VICReg coordinate-wise variance hinge ($\text{var\_weight} \ge 1.0$) and covariance penalty ($\text{cov\_weight} \ge 1.0$) are required to maintain a healthy $\approx 6.0\text{D}$ intrinsic dimension on imbalanced sound-metal grids.
- **Strict Prohibition of Software Differential Probing (Diffensor / Differential Coil Emulation)**: The dataset consists of single absolute sensor measurements (Hall Air Core, Hall Pot Core, TMR). We do NOT have differential hardware coils. NEVER propose or implement artificial spatial differentiation, adjacent probe subtraction ($h_{\text{center}} - \bar{h}_{\text{ring}}$), or spatial Laplacian filtering to mimic hardware differential probes. Such ad-hoc manipulations do not learn the intrinsic physical diffusion of PECT, destroy absolute transient field dynamics, amplify high-frequency scanner noise, and are permanently banned.

## 4. Single Unified Foundation Model Mandate
- **Single Checkpoint Across All Downstream Tasks**: The core mission is to construct a single, task-agnostic foundation model for PECT. The representation must serve defect detection (AUC, AP, CNR), quantitative physical sizing (depth, diameter, volume), and cross-sensor/cross-waveform generalization simultaneously from a single universal representation $Z_{\text{foundation}} \in \mathbb{R}^{2D}$. NEVER propose or accept task-specific model fragmentation (e.g., using Model A for detection and Model B for sizing).
- **Full-Rank Dual-Subspace Preservation (No Zero-Sum Competition)**: The incident diffusion carrier field ($\Phi_{\text{carrier}}$) and the localized defect scattering perturbation ($\Phi_{\text{scattering}}$) are independent physical components ($x_{\text{total}} = x_{\text{inc}} + \Delta x$). NEVER force them into competing zero-sum convex gates ($(1-g) \cdot \Phi_{\text{carrier}} + g \cdot \Phi_{\text{scattering}}$) that artificially starve carrier contrast. Both subspaces must be preserved at full rank.
- **Hardware-Agnostic Self-Calibration**: Hardware transfer function offsets across disparate sensors (Hall Air, Hall Pot, TMR) must be handled via unsupervised spatial self-calibration (zero-centering against natural $>98\%$ sound metal), preserving full dynamic range without corrupting pretraining manifolds with artificial cross-sensor penalties.
